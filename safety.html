Safety Center

SpeakUp connects you with strangers — a few rules keep it safe for everyone:

  • Never share passwords, bank details, or money with someone you met on a call.
  • Never share your exact address or workplace with a stranger.
  • Use Report and Block the moment something feels wrong — every report is reviewed.
  • You can end any call instantly, no explanation needed.
  • SpeakUp is for adults 18+ only.

Reports are typically reviewed within 24 hours. Repeat or severe violations result in an immediate suspension pending review.
